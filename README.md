# Historical Earnings Move, ATM Straddle Pricing, and Accuracy of Post-Earnings Moves

An empirical research project designed to evaluate noteworthy patterns in historical earnings data.
Key analysis such as: 
  - Historical earnings move persistence.
  - Systematic bias of historical earnings data.
  - How well does historical earnings move predict future realized moves on an event-by-event basis?

The study also delved into the relationship between historical earnings data and the option market's implied expected moves. 
Key questions such as: 
  - How correlated is a stock's historical post-earnings move and the option market's implied move?
  - How do live earnings outcomes perform when implied move diverges significantly from historical move?
